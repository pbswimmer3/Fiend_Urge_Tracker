# Privacy Policy — Fiend

_Last updated: v1.0_

Fiend is a self-awareness journal for tracking urges and recovery. Privacy is the primary design constraint.

## What we collect

Fiend stores the following **on the device only**:

- Urge log entries: timestamp, intensity (1–5), selected emotional tags, optional notes
- Optional GPS coordinates attached to an urge (only if you grant "While Using" location permission)
- Slip entries: timestamp and required reflection text
- Profile preferences: habit name, clean start date, sober-dividend settings, onboarding state

## What we do not do

- We do **not** transmit any of the above off-device
- We do **not** operate a backend, analytics pipeline, or telemetry service
- We do **not** embed third-party SDKs, trackers, advertising identifiers, or crash reporters
- We do **not** sell, share, or monetize your data in any form
- We do **not** enable iCloud sync unless a future version adds it as a clearly-labeled opt-in feature

## Your controls

- Revoke location access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Fiend
- "Erase all data" in Settings removes all urges and slips immediately
- Deleting the app deletes the SwiftData store entirely

## Children

Fiend is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app, delete the app to remove all local data.

## Changes to this policy

Future versions that introduce optional cloud sync, export, or sharing will update this policy and require explicit in-app consent before enabling any new data flow.
